1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to the website owner.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep this website running reliably, to understand how visitors interact with it, and to improve its content and performance over time. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary cookies – required for the website’s core functionality, such as remembering your cookie preferences and enabling secure navigation. These cannot be switched off.
  • Performance & analytics cookies – help us understand how visitors use this website (for example, which pages are most visited) so we can improve it. Data is generally collected in an aggregated, non-identifying form.
  • Functional cookies – remember choices you make on the website to provide a more personalised experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on this website may be set by third-party services we use, such as font or analytics providers. These third parties may use cookies in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies already stored on your device. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of this website.

You can typically manage cookie settings in your browser under “Settings” or “Privacy” – the exact steps vary depending on which browser you use.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for legal and regulatory reasons. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
