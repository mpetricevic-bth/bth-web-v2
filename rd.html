Advanced Virtual Power Plant

Smart energy management at portfolio scale. aVPP is an advanced virtual power plant solution that combines cloud technology, artificial intelligence, machine learning and advanced optimization algorithms to enable automated management of distributed energy resources. The solution supports portfolio optimization, forecasting, centralized asset management, real-time monitoring and participation in energy and ancillary service markets.

Key features

  • Portfolio monitoring
  • AI-powered forecasting
  • Market participation
  • Energy trading optimization
  • Flexible asset dispatch
  • Performance analytics

UKUS - Universal Communication & Control System

Connecting industrial systems through smarter communication and control. UKUS is a research and development project focused on creating a universal communication and control platform for industrial plants, with a particular focus on energy facilities. The solution integrates communication and control modules, centralizes plant data and enables advanced analysis and process optimization. Support for different industrial communication protocols allows the platform to connect a wide range of systems and equipment within a unified environment.

Key capabilities

  • Industrial communication
  • Centralized data management
  • Process optimization
  • Data acquisition & analytics
  • System integration
  • Decision support & control
