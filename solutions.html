01/ General

Advanced Management & Control Solutions

Brodotehna develops advanced software and control solutions for hydropower and power generation, battery energy storage systems (BESS) and pumped storage, virtual power plants, microgrids, and industrial systems. Our solutions combine automation, forecasting, optimization, and real-time monitoring to help operators improve reliability, efficiency, and system performance.

Advanced Virtual Power Plant

Advanced Microgrid Controller

A control solution for microgrids that supports optimized energy flow, forecasting, dispatch and stable operation in grid-connected or islanded mode.

Hydro, Pumped Storage & Battery Energy Storage Optimization

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition

We design and develop fully customized SCADA solutions tailored to each customer's process and operational requirements. From concept to commissioning, we deliver complete, reliable monitoring and control systems.

aHydroControl

Advanced turbine regulation and automated unit control for hydropower plants, combining governor control, start/stop sequences, synchronization, and auxiliary system management on a reliable PLC platform.

aBoiler

Intelligent boiler and combustion control for biomass and CHP plants, combining fuel and air regulation, process sequencing, and auxiliary system management on a robust PLC platform.
